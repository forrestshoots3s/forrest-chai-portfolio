## Strat Market Radar

Production competitive-intelligence agents that write to a shared monthly Feishu sheet, with tabs for X, WeChat official accounts, TechCrunch, official sites and third-party channels, and AI film & IP news.

- **Daily runtime.** Every run starts cold from a versioned release package with a SHA-256 manifest, so it never depends on chat history or leftover files. It pulls X, WeChat, and TechCrunch through primary APIs with per-account fallbacks, over a 24-hour main window plus a 72-hour late-recovery sweep.
- **Correctness gates.** If source coverage or pagination completeness drops below 95%, the production write is blocked. Unknowns stay visible as warnings instead of being reported as "no update", and items that fail to parse go to a manual-review tab.
- **Verified writes.** Each run builds a deterministic write plan, writes, reads the columns back, and uploads an audit bundle. Zero duplicates per tab and zero post-write mismatches are completion criteria.
- **Weekly official-site layer.** A Firecrawl-based runtime covers 49 official-site entry points across 19 companies plus ~70 AI film & IP sources, gap-filling through RSS, sitemaps, and JSON-LD, with fingerprint-based dedup that keeps multiple changelog events per page.
- **Results.** On 42 labeled events, the stateful runtime (checkpoints plus idempotent writes) raised recall from 32% to 95% with zero duplicates.
- **Source registry.** Maintained the sources behind all of it: ~150 competitor products, ~40 domestic media and VC accounts, and ~70 overseas labs, investors, and creators.

## Model Feedback Platform

- Python platform that collects and analyzes 100K+ posts and comments on frontier video models, including Seedance 2.5, at 1K+ API calls per week, with source-aware concurrency, adaptive backoff, and budget controls.
- Classifies and ranks feedback by relevance, sentiment, theme, and engagement, cutting synthesis time by about 90% and surfacing recurring issues in instruction following, controllability, and consistency.

## Market Data & Weekly Briefs

- Incremental Python/SQL ETL across 30+ AI products, joining Sensor Tower revenue and DAU with web traffic, launch, and funding data through normalized schemas, per-source watermarks, and replayable checkpoints; data prep time dropped about 75%.
- Weekly AI creative-tools industry brief: usage trends across ~35 products, plus track, funding, people, and product news.
- Tuesday competitor brief for the Dreamina content meeting, with rule-based growth attribution: any metric up more than 5% week over week gets a primary and secondary cause, a confidence level, and sources.
- Bi-weekly Web and App MAU/DAU benchmark across ~50 products for CapCut.
